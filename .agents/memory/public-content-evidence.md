---
name: Public content evidence
description: Evidence and imagery rules for public-facing Esoko sector content.
---

Public sector and industry pages must not introduce new customer claims, country lists, operational statistics, certifications, or case studies without a verified source. Reuse figures already published elsewhere on the Esoko site or load evidence from the Resources CMS.

**Why:** Public pages need to feel credible without implying capabilities or impact that the project cannot substantiate.

**How to apply:** Prefer authentic field photography supplied for the project over generic stock or synthetic documentary images. When no verified customer story exists, show a clear CMS-aware empty state instead of placeholder success stories.
