Public sector, industry, and Company pages must not introduce new customer claims, country lists, impact or operational statistics, certifications, biographies, milestones, awards, or case studies without a verified source. Reuse facts already published elsewhere on the Esoko site or load evidence from the Resources CMS.

**Why:** Public pages need to feel credible without implying capabilities or impact that the project cannot substantiate.

**How to apply:** Prefer authentic field photography supplied for the project over generic stock or synthetic documentary images. Use qualitative, factual copy when figures or biographies are unverified. When no verified customer story exists, show a clear CMS-aware empty state instead of placeholder success stories.

The user approved the “A quiet signal” design for only the Next.js preview's hero and first two sections. Retain the shared site navigation and all later homepage sections. This is an intentional exception to original-site parity, not permission for a broader redesign or publication.

**Why:** The approval explicitly limited integration to this opening. Restoring the original carousel for parity would undo that approved decision.

**How to apply:** Keep the approved opening when maintaining migration parity elsewhere. Design approval does not grant marketing consent for face-flagged field photos; planned portrait placements remain marked until filename-specific consent is confirmed. Do not identify anonymous photographs as the quoted farmer.
