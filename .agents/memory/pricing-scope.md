---
name: Pricing scope
description: Commercial content constraints for Esoko pricing and sales enquiries.
---

Esoko uses custom enterprise quotes. Keep public pricing content contact-to-quote; do not invent fixed prices, packaged tier limits, contract terms, discounts, or service guarantees.

**Why:** The requested pricing page is an enterprise enquiry page, not a self-service catalogue or payment flow.

**How to apply:** Explain the engagement qualitatively and use the existing demo enquiry flow for Contact Sales until verified commercial details are supplied.
