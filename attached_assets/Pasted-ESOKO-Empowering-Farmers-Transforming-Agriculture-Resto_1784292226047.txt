WHO WE ARE
Who is Esoko?

Founded in 2008, Esoko is one of Africa's leading Agritech companies dedicated to transforming agricultural markets through technology.

We help organizations collect high-quality data, deliver critical information, deploy field operations, connect farmers to markets, and build resilient agricultural ecosystems across Africa.

Through innovative mobile technologies, artificial intelligence, cloud platforms, and digital communication tools, Esoko supports governments, NGOs, agribusinesses, financial institutions, and development partners in creating measurable social and economic impact.

Design Direction

To position Esoko as a premium enterprise technology company, the visual design should emphasize trust, innovation, and scale:

Visual style: Modern SaaS aesthetic inspired by Salesforce, Stripe, Microsoft, and Vercel, with subtle agritech elements.
Color palette: Deep Forest Green (#0B6B3A), Emerald (#16A34A), Earth Gold (#F4B400), Sky Blue (#0EA5E9), White (#FFFFFF), Charcoal (#1F2937), and Light Gray (#F8FAFC).
Typography: Inter or Manrope for body text, with Space Grotesk or Plus Jakarta Sans for headings.
Components: Rounded cards (20–24 px radius), glassmorphism accents, soft shadows, animated counters, interactive maps, AI dashboard previews, and smooth scroll-triggered animations.
Motion: Framer Motion for page transitions, hover effects, parallax hero imagery, animated gradients, and interactive data visualizations.
Responsiveness: Mobile-first with optimized navigation, touch-friendly interactions, and accessibility compliant (WCAG 2.1 AA).

This structure elevates Esoko from a traditional agritech website to a modern digital agriculture platform that clearly communicates its enterprise capabilities while remaining approachable for governments, NGOs, development partners, and commercial agribusinesses.
