Create a premium, modern Careers platform for Esoko that positions the company as one of Africa's most innovative technology employers.

The Careers experience should not look like a simple job listing page. It should feel like a world-class employer brand website similar to Microsoft Careers, Google Careers, Salesforce Careers, Stripe Careers, and Airbnb Careers.

The purpose is to attract world-class talent across software engineering, AI, agriculture technology, data science, product management, operations, climate technology, and business functions.
